What changed from 12 Oct

  1. HE #2 Pause keeps your place. The playhead stays on the beat where you paused (dashed), the button reads "Resume", and it carries on from there. Playhead speed follows the tempo.
  2. HE #2 "Finish attempt" renamed "End take" to match Iter2.03 Change 2. "Attempt" renamed "take" throughout.
  3. HE #1 Every take shows "Autosaved". The latest take has "Play back" and "Discard take" (with confirmation), and discarding undoes its effect on the goal and the coach.
  4. HE #9 Live input-level meter while recording, with a "Voice picked up" label (simulated).
  5. HE #8 A missed take says why: timing ("came in late after the rest") or pitch ("beat 3 was flat"). History chips show the reason too.
  6. HE #5 Help stays top-right and now runs a step-by-step overlay that dims the screen, outlines each control and puts the tip beside it.
  7. HE #17 (A only) Tap the BPM value to type an exact tempo (48 to 96). The slider now moves in 1 BPM steps; − / + still jump 5%.
  8. HE #20 No dead buttons. Back and "Continue" show a message that they're outside this prototype.

Unchanged: the MODERATOR ONLY judging panel (now with timing / pitch reasons). It still needs a team member, which conflicts with the brief's "automated, no team intervention" rule. Decide before Iter2.05.
